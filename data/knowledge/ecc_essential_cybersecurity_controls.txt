Document: Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-2:2024) - National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA), Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
Overview: The ECC consist of 4 main domains, 21 subdomains, and 108 main controls (with sub-controls). They set the minimum cybersecurity requirements for government agencies, their affiliated companies, and private entities owning/operating/hosting Critical National Infrastructure (CNI) in Saudi Arabia. Protection goals: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability. Pillars: Strategy, People, Process, Technology.

===== Domain 1: Cybersecurity Governance =====

Subdomain 1-1 Cybersecurity Strategy
Objective: Ensure the entity's action plans, objectives, initiatives, and projects contribute to compliance with relevant legislative and regulatory requirements.
1-1-1 The cybersecurity strategy shall be identified, documented, approved, and supported by the Authorized Official, aligned with relevant legislative and regulatory requirements.
1-1-2 The entity shall execute an action plan to apply the cybersecurity strategy.
1-1-3 The cybersecurity strategy shall be reviewed at planned intervals or upon changes to relevant requirements.

Subdomain 1-2 Cybersecurity Management
Objective: Ensure the Authorized Official complies with and supports implementation and management of cybersecurity programs.
1-2-1 A cybersecurity department shall be established, independent from IT/Communications Department, ideally reporting directly to the head of the entity.
1-2-2 All cybersecurity positions shall be filled with full-time and qualified Saudi cybersecurity professionals.
1-2-3 A cybersecurity supervisory committee shall be established with identified members, responsibilities, and governance framework, including the head of the cybersecurity department.

Subdomain 1-3 Cybersecurity Policies and Procedures
Objective: Ensure cybersecurity requirements and compliance are documented and communicated.
1-3-1 Cybersecurity policies and procedures, including controls and requirements, shall be identified, documented, approved, and communicated.
1-3-2 The cybersecurity department shall ensure implementation of policies and procedures.
1-3-3 Policies and procedures shall be supported by technical security standards (e.g., firewall, database, OS standards).
1-3-4 Policies and procedures shall be reviewed and updated at planned intervals or upon changes; changes documented and approved.

Subdomain 1-4 Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities
Objective: Ensure roles and responsibilities are clearly defined for all parties implementing cybersecurity controls.
1-4-1 The Authorized Official shall identify, document, and approve the governance organizational structure, roles, and responsibilities.
1-4-2 Roles and responsibilities shall be reviewed and updated at planned intervals or upon changes.

Subdomain 1-5 Cybersecurity Risk Management
Objective: Ensure managing cybersecurity risks methodologically to protect information and technology assets.
1-5-1 The cybersecurity risk management methodology and procedures shall be identified, documented, and approved.
1-5-2 The methodology and procedures shall be implemented.
1-5-3 Risk assessment shall be implemented at least: (1-5-3-1) at early stage of technology projects; (1-5-3-2) before major changes to technology infrastructure; (1-5-3-3) during planning to obtain third-party services; (1-5-3-4) during planning and before release of new technology services/products.
1-5-4 The methodology and procedures shall be reviewed and updated periodically.

Subdomain 1-6 Cybersecurity in Information and Technology Project Management
Objective: Ensure cybersecurity requirements are included in project management methodology to protect confidentiality, integrity, accuracy, and availability.
1-6-1 Cybersecurity requirements shall be included in project management methodology and change management.
1-6-2 Requirements for project/change management shall include at minimum: (1-6-2-1) vulnerability assessment and remediation; (1-6-2-2) reviewing secure configuration, hardening, and update packages before launching projects/changes.
1-6-3 Requirements for software/application development shall include at minimum: (1-6-3-1) secure coding standards; (1-6-3-2) trusted/licensed sources for development tools and libraries; (1-6-3-3) compliance testing against cybersecurity requirements; (1-6-3-4) secure integration between applications; (1-6-3-5) reviewing secure configuration/hardening and updates before launching software products.
1-6-4 Project management cybersecurity requirements shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 1-7 Compliance with Cybersecurity Standards, Laws and Regulations
Objective: Ensure the entity's cybersecurity program complies with relevant legislative and regulatory requirements.
1-7-1 If nationally approved international agreements/commitments include cybersecurity requirements, the entity shall identify and comply with them.

Subdomain 1-8 Periodical Cybersecurity Review and Audit
Objective: Ensure cybersecurity controls are implemented and applicable per policies, national and international requirements.
1-8-1 The cybersecurity department shall periodically review implementation of cybersecurity controls.
1-8-2 Implementation shall also be reviewed/audited by parties other than the cybersecurity department, independently, per Generally Accepted Auditing Standards (GAAS).
1-8-3 Results of audits/reviews shall be documented and presented to the supervisory committee and Authorized Official, including scope, observations, recommendations, corrective actions, and remediation plans.

Subdomain 1-9 Cybersecurity in Human Resources
Objective: Ensure cybersecurity risks and requirements for personnel are managed efficiently before, during, and after employment.
1-9-1 Requirements for personnel shall be identified, documented, and approved for all employment stages.
1-9-2 Requirements for personnel shall be implemented.
1-9-3 Pre-employment requirements shall include at minimum: (1-9-3-1) non-disclosure and cybersecurity responsibility clauses in contracts; (1-9-3-2) screening/vetting for cybersecurity and privileged technical positions.
1-9-4 During-employment requirements shall include at minimum: (1-9-4-1) cybersecurity awareness during onboarding and employment; (1-9-4-2) implementation and compliance with cybersecurity policies/procedures/operations.
1-9-5 Personnel access/powers shall be reviewed and revoked immediately upon end/termination of employment.
1-9-6 Personnel cybersecurity requirements shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 1-10 Cybersecurity Awareness and Training Program
Objective: Ensure personnel have required security awareness, know their responsibilities, and are equipped with necessary skills.
1-10-1 An awareness program delivered through multiple channels shall be periodically developed and approved.
1-10-2 The approved program shall be implemented.
1-10-3 The program shall cover at minimum: (1-10-3-1) secure handling of email/phishing; (1-10-3-2) secure handling of mobile devices/storage media; (1-10-3-3) secure internet browsing; (1-10-3-4) secure usage of social media.
1-10-4 Specialized training shall be provided per role: (1-10-4-1) cybersecurity department personnel; (1-10-4-2) software/application developers and IT asset operators; (1-10-4-3) executive/supervisory positions.
1-10-5 Implementation of the awareness program shall be periodically reviewed.

===== Domain 2: Cybersecurity Defense =====

Subdomain 2-1 Asset Management
Objective: Ensure an accurate, updated inventory of information and technology assets to maintain confidentiality, integrity, accuracy, and availability.
2-1-1 Cybersecurity requirements for asset management shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-1-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-1-3 The acceptable use policy for assets shall be identified, documented, approved, and communicated.
2-1-4 The acceptable use policy shall be implemented.
2-1-5 Assets shall be classified, labeled, and handled per relevant legislative and regulatory requirements.
2-1-6 Asset management requirements shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-2 Identity and Access Management
Objective: Ensure protection of logical access to prevent unauthorized access and restrict access to necessary scope.
2-2-1 Identity and access management requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-2-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-2-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-2-3-1) single-factor authentication (username/password); (2-2-3-2) multi-factor authentication for remote access and privileged accounts; (2-2-3-3) user authorization based on need-to-know, need-to-use, least privilege, segregation of duties; (2-2-3-4) privileged access management; (2-2-3-5) periodic review of identities and access rights.
2-2-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-3 Information System and Information Processing Facilities Protection
Objective: Ensure protection of information systems and processing facilities, including workstations and infrastructure, against cyber risks.
2-3-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-3-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-3-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-3-3-1) protection from viruses/malware on workstations and servers using modern technologies; (2-3-3-2) strict restriction on external storage media use; (2-3-3-3) patch management for systems/applications/devices; (2-3-3-4) centralized clock synchronization with an accurate trusted source (e.g., SASO).
2-3-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-4 Email Protection
Objective: Ensure protection of the entity's email service against cyber risks.
2-4-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-4-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-4-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-4-3-1) analyzing/filtering phishing and spam emails; (2-4-3-2) multi-factor authentication for remote/webmail access; (2-4-3-3) email archiving and backup; (2-4-3-4) protection against Advanced Persistent Threats (APT)/zero-day malware; (2-4-3-5) domain validation via SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
2-4-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-5 Network Security Management
Objective: Ensure protection of the entity's networks against cyber risks.
2-5-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-5-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-5-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-5-3-1) logical/physical network segmentation using firewall and defense-in-depth; (2-5-3-2) isolation of production network from test/dev environments; (2-5-3-3) secure browsing/internet connectivity with restrictions on suspicious sites; (2-5-3-4) wireless network security and encryption; (2-5-3-5) restricting/managing network ports, protocols, and services; (2-5-3-6) Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS); (2-5-3-7) DNS security; (2-5-3-8) protection of internet browsing channel against APT; (2-5-3-9) protection against DDoS attacks.
2-5-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-6 Mobile Devices Security
Objective: Ensure protection of mobile devices (laptops, smartphones, tablets) and secure BYOD handling.
2-6-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-6-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-6-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-6-3-1) separation/encryption of data on mobile devices/BYOD; (2-6-3-2) controlled/restricted use per business interest; (2-6-3-3) deletion of data upon device loss or employment termination; (2-6-3-4) security awareness for users.
2-6-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-7 Data and Information Protection
Objective: Ensure confidentiality, integrity, accuracy, and availability of data and information.
2-7-1 Requirements for protecting/handling data shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-7-2 Requirements shall be implemented based on data classification level.
2-7-3 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-8 Cryptography
Objective: Ensure proper and efficient use of cryptography to protect electronic information assets.
2-8-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-8-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-8-3 Requirements shall include at minimum the National Cryptographic Standards (published by NCA): (2-8-3-1) approved cryptographic systems/solutions standards and restrictions; (2-8-3-2) secure management of cryptographic keys during their lifecycle; (2-8-3-3) encryption of data in-transit and at-rest per classification.
2-8-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-9 Backup and Recovery Management
Objective: Ensure protection of data, information, and technical configurations against cyber risks via backups.
2-9-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-9-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-9-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-9-3-1) backup scope covering critical technology/information assets; (2-9-3-2) ability to quickly recover data/systems after incidents; (2-9-3-3) periodic testing of backup recovery effectiveness.
2-9-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-10 Vulnerabilities Management
Objective: Ensure timely detection and effective remediation of technical vulnerabilities.
2-10-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-10-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-10-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-10-3-1) periodic vulnerability assessment/detection; (2-10-3-2) vulnerability classification by severity; (2-10-3-3) remediation based on classification and cyber risk; (2-10-3-4) patch management verified in a non-production environment before deployment; (2-10-3-5) subscription with trusted sources for new vulnerabilities.
2-10-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-11 Penetration Testing
Objective: Assess and test cybersecurity defense capabilities via simulated attacks to discover unknown weaknesses.
2-11-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-11-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-11-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-11-3-1) scope covering all externally provided services (infrastructure, websites, web apps, mobile apps, email, remote access); (2-11-3-2) conducting penetration tests periodically.
2-11-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-12 Cybersecurity Event Logs and Monitoring Management
Objective: Ensure timely collection, analysis, and monitoring of event logs for proactive detection of cyber-attacks.
2-12-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-12-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-12-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-12-3-1) activation of event logs for critical information assets; (2-12-3-2) activation of logs for privileged/remote access accounts; (2-12-3-3) SIEM techniques for log collection; (2-12-3-4) continuous monitoring of event logs; (2-12-3-5) retention period of at least 12 months.
2-12-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-13 Cybersecurity Incident and Threat Management
Objective: Ensure timely identification, detection, and effective management of cybersecurity incidents and proactive response to threats.
2-13-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-13-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-13-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-13-3-1) incident response plans and escalation procedures; (2-13-3-2) incident classification; (2-13-3-3) reporting incidents to NCA; (2-13-3-4) sharing incident notifications/threat intelligence/indicators/reports with NCA; (2-13-3-5) collecting and handling threat intelligence feeds.
2-13-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-14 Physical Security
Objective: Ensure protection of information and technology assets against unauthorized physical access, loss, theft, and damage.
2-14-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-14-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-14-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-14-3-1) authorized access to critical areas (data center, DR center, security surveillance center, network rooms); (2-14-3-2) access and monitoring logs (CCTV); (2-14-3-3) protection of access/monitoring log information; (2-14-3-4) secure destruction/re-use of physical assets holding classified information; (2-14-3-5) security of devices/equipment inside and outside facilities.
2-14-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 2-15 Web Application Security
Objective: Ensure protection of external web applications against cyber risks.
2-15-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
2-15-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
2-15-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (2-15-3-1) web application firewall; (2-15-3-2) multi-tier architecture; (2-15-3-3) secure protocols (e.g., HTTPS); (2-15-3-4) clarified secure usage policy for users; (2-15-3-5) user authentication with appropriate factors based on risk assessment.
2-15-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

===== Domain 3: Cybersecurity Resilience =====

Subdomain 3-1 Cybersecurity Resilience Aspects of Business Continuity Management (BCM)
Objective: Ensure cybersecurity resilience requirements are included in business continuity management to minimize impacts of disruptions on critical e-services caused by cyber risks.
3-1-1 Requirements shall be identified, documented, and approved.
3-1-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
3-1-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (3-1-3-1) ensuring continuity of cybersecurity systems/procedures; (3-1-3-2) developing response plans for incidents affecting business continuity; (3-1-3-3) developing disaster recovery plans.
3-1-4 Implementation shall be periodically reviewed.

===== Domain 4: Third-Party and Cloud Computing Cybersecurity =====

Subdomain 4-1 Third-Party Cybersecurity
Objective: Ensure protection of the entity's assets against third-party cybersecurity risks (IT outsourcing, cybersecurity outsourcing, managed services).
4-1-1 Requirements for contracts/agreements with third parties shall be identified, documented, and approved.
4-1-2 Requirements for contracts/SLAs shall include at minimum: (4-1-2-1) non-disclosure and secure data removal clauses upon service end; (4-1-2-2) communication procedures in case of a cybersecurity incident; (4-1-2-3) obligating third parties to apply the entity's cybersecurity requirements/policies and relevant laws.
4-1-3 Requirements for IT/cybersecurity outsourcing or managed services contracts shall include at minimum: (4-1-3-1) conducting a cybersecurity risk assessment before signing contracts or upon regulatory changes; (4-1-3-2) managed service centers using remote access shall be fully located within Saudi Arabia.
4-1-4 Third-party requirements shall be periodically reviewed.

Subdomain 4-2 Cloud Computing and Hosting Cybersecurity
Objective: Ensure proper remediation of cyber risks and implementation of cybersecurity requirements for cloud computing and hosting, protecting assets hosted/processed/managed by third parties.
4-2-1 Requirements for use of cloud computing/hosting services shall be identified, documented, and approved.
4-2-2 Requirements shall be implemented.
4-2-3 Requirements shall include at minimum: (4-2-3-1) protection of entity's data by cloud/hosting providers per classification level, and returning data in usable format upon service completion; (4-2-3-2) separation of the entity's environment (especially virtual servers) from other entities' environments within the cloud provider.
4-2-4 Requirements shall be periodically reviewed.
